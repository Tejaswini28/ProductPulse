## Bank Account Management FAQ

### Why can't I use a bank account I just added?
Newly added bank accounts may require verification before they can be used for payments. If verification has not completed successfully, the account may remain unavailable.

### Why did bank verification fail?
Verification may fail because account information could not be verified or because the verification service was temporarily unavailable. Try again later if you received a temporary service error.

### Why can't I remove a saved bank account?
A saved bank account may not be removable while an active payment depends on it.

## Payment Flex FAQ

### Why don't I see Payment Flex?
Payment Flex is available only when the account meets current eligibility requirements. Not seeing an offer does not always mean something is broken.

### Why are some payment dates unavailable?
Payment Flex provides eligible payment dates based on product and account rules. Some calendar dates may not be selectable.

### Will the app tell me why a date is unavailable?
The experience may not always provide a detailed reason for each unavailable date. If another date is available, you can select that date and continue.

## Balance Assist Plan FAQ

### When is my Balance Assist Plan active?
Your plan is active after you select the plan terms, even if the payment schedule has not yet been successfully created.

### What happens if scheduling fails?
A temporary scheduling error may prevent setup from completing. If you receive an error, try again later. A successful confirmation indicates that the payment schedule was created.

### Can I review my scheduled payments later?
Yes. After a plan and its payment schedule are successfully created, you can review the existing schedule in the plan-management experience.
